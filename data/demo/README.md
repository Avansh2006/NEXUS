# Synthetic evidence only

All names, locations and identifiers are fictional. SYN-PHONE and SYN-ACCOUNT
identifiers intentionally cannot be dialed or routed; they are accepted only as
explicit synthetic tokens. ZZ registrations and `.invalid` UPI domains are fixtures.
Production-format phone validators are tested separately without demo contact data.
Regenerate using `python scripts/generate_demo.py` (seed 42).
Ground truth and 18 independently specified gold FIRs are committed with output.
